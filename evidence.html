The evidence

Designed to be measured, before it asks to be believed

Healthy Hectare's credibility rests on its measurement design: what it tracks, how it compares, and why the result will be evidence rather than anecdote. The baseline study is in planning — this page describes the framework, not results.

The framework

What the programme will measure

Five categories, tracked on the same parcel over the full four-year cycle. Each is chosen because it matters commercially as well as scientifically.

M-01 · Soil

Soil health & carbon

Soil function and resilience over time — the asset the whole model is designed to build.

M-02 · Inputs

Chemical input reduction

Reduction — and eventual elimination — of highly hazardous pesticides in transition areas, alongside the overall chemical burden.

M-03 · Residues

Residue levels

Pesticide residues on produce, tracked against the baseline as practices change.

M-04 · Production

Yield & quality stability

Whether yield, quality, and plant performance under stress hold or improve through transition.

M-05 · Economics

Comparative cost efficiency

Input costs and economic efficiency relative to conventional practice on the same farm, season on season.

Output

Research-grade reporting

Findings translated into reporting and transition-pathway knowledge the wider sector can use.

Why it's credible

Same farm, same conditions — like for like

Most agricultural claims fail on comparability: different farms, different seasons, different management. Healthy Hectare removes that weakness by design — the comparison happens on one farm, between a defined transition parcel and continuing conventional practice, from a shared baseline.

Study design — illustrative, not results

Parcel A — reference

Continuing practice

The farm's existing programme continues unchanged, providing the live comparison line.

Parcel B — transition

Healthy Hectare parcel

A defined parcel transitions to biological and regenerative practice with expert support and close monitoring.

Baseline Season 1 Season 2 Season 3 Season 4

Both parcels are measured against the same baseline, across all five categories, every season. The output is a like-for-like record of what transition actually does — to soil, inputs, residues, production, and cost.

Programme status

The baseline study is in planning at the programme's first pilot site. No measurement results are reported yet — and none will be published here until they are real, reviewed, and attributable. What you see above is the design the programme holds itself to.

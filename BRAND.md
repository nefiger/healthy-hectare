# Healthy Hectare — Visual Identity (DRAFT)

> **Status: draft, pending sign-off.** This identity — palette, typography, and wordmark —
> was proposed as part of the website build and is a genuine design proposal, not a locked
> decision. Per the ecosystem convention (see ABA's BRAND.md), Anna is the final arbiter;
> nothing here should be treated as final until she signs it off.

## Design intent

Healthy Hectare's positioning is "industry analyst, not pleading activist": a benchmark
programme whose credibility comes from measurement design. The identity is built around
one idea — **measured land**. Where ABA's system is warm and advocacy-forward (parchment,
terracotta, forest green), HH runs cooler and more precise: deeper greens, survey-mark
motifs, monospaced measurement labels. Sibling, not twin.

## The mark

A square parcel — one hectare — divided into crop rows, with **one row in ochre: the row
in transition**. That's the whole programme in a glyph: a defined parcel, mostly unchanged,
one measured strip moving first.

The mark is drawn as inline SVG in the site header/footer and as `assets/img/favicon.svg`.
The lockup pairs the mark with "Healthy Hectare" set in Fraunces SemiBold.

| **Midnight Field** | `#0F1F17` | Darkest ground — footer, dark bands, hero overlays |
| **Benchmark Green** | `#1E4D38` | Primary — headings, buttons, the HH column |
| **Row Sage** | `#93A98B` | Secondary text on dark grounds |
| **Moss** | `#5C6F58` | Muted text/captions on light grounds |
| **Harvest Ochre** | `#C9973B` | Accent — the "transition row", eyebrows on dark, rules, marks |
| **Deep Ochre** | `#9C742A` | Ochre adjusted for legibility on light grounds |
| **Turned Earth** | `#57422E` | Sparing support tone (soil content, "standard" column) |

Relationship to ABA: same warm-neutral light grounds and green-family primary so the two
sit together as siblings, but HH's green is cooler and deeper than ABA's `#2D5016`, and
HH's accent is ochre where ABA's is terracotta — adjacent, not identical.

## Typography

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / headings | **Fraunces** (SemiBold 600) | A serif with more bite than ABA's Lora — editorial, precise, its own voice |
| Body / UI | **Inter** (400/500/600) | Cooler and more clinical than ABA's Poppins |
| Measurement labels | **IBM Plex Mono** (400/500) | Eyebrows, metric IDs (`M-01 · SOIL`), status tags, figure captions — the "field notebook" register |

All via Google Fonts (same sourcing pattern as ABA).

## Motifs

- **Ruler ticks** — the `rule-ticks` section divider: a hairline with survey-tick marks,
  the benchmark/measurement idea as a graphic element.
- **Mono eyebrows and metric IDs** — every section is labelled like an entry in a study
  protocol (`M-01`, `PRESSURE 03`, `PARCEL B — TRANSITION`).
- **Parcel diagrams** — comparisons drawn as labelled parcels, never as charts that could
  be mistaken for results.
- **Photography** — aerial row-crop imagery as the signature (rows = measurement lines at
  landscape scale). Current images are licensed stock, flagged on-site as placeholders
  pending real pilot photography.

## What this identity deliberately avoids

- Faux data dashboards or chart styling that implies tracked results
- Activist visual language (badges, slogans, exclamation marks)
- Direct reuse of ABA's palette/type (the old brochure borrowed it as an expedient;
  this replaces that)
